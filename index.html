01 · APPROACH

SEO Consulting Built Around
Your Website's Real Problems.

SEO is not simply adding keywords to a page and waiting for rankings. A strong SEO strategy starts by understanding the business, the audience, the website, the competitive landscape and the search intent behind the queries that matter.

My approach combines technical SEO, keyword research, search intent analysis, on-page optimization, content SEO, local SEO, off-page analysis, internal linking, structured data and AI-assisted research.

As an SEO consultant in Bangladesh, I work with a practical, evidence-first mindset. The objective isn't to make unsupported ranking promises — it's to identify what's preventing a website from performing well, prioritize the highest-impact opportunities, and create a clear path for improvement.

Off-page SEO is one part of a broader SEO strategy alongside technical and on-page work, not a standalone guarantee of rankings. Third-party metrics like Domain Authority or Domain Rating are useful directional tools from SEO vendors, not confirmed Google ranking factors. This work follows Google's own guidance on link spam policies and avoids PBNs, link farms, automated or paid links intended to manipulate rankings, spammy directories, comment/forum spam and manipulative guest posting.

06 · FOUNDATIONS

Technical SEO Comes First.

A beautiful website cannot compensate for serious crawlability, indexing, architecture or technical problems. Google's own guidance is clear that pages need to be accessible to crawlers, return a successful HTTP response, and contain indexable content to even be eligible for indexing.

That's why technical SEO is one of the core areas of my work — see the Technical SEO service page for the full breakdown.

15 · FAQ

Common questions, answered directly.

A quick preview — see the full FAQ page for the complete list.

What does an SEO consultant do?

An SEO consultant audits a website's technical health, content and off-page signals, then builds a prioritized plan to improve how the site is crawled, indexed and matched to search intent.

What is technical SEO?

Technical SEO is the foundation work that helps search engines crawl, render and index a site correctly: crawlability, indexability, site architecture, canonical tags, redirects, structured data and Core Web Vitals.

How long does SEO take to show results?

It depends on the site's starting point, competition and how fast fixes are implemented. There are no guaranteed timelines or guaranteed rankings.
