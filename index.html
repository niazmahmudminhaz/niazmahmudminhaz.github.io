AI Search, AEO and GEO

Search is expanding beyond the traditional ten-blue-links model. AI search SEO focuses on making information clear enough for systems to interpret, retrieve and summarize responsibly. AEO, or Answer Engine Optimization, emphasizes direct answers and question-led content. GEO, or Generative Engine Optimization, focuses on visibility within generative search experiences. These areas do not replace technical SEO or useful content. They build on the same fundamentals: clear information architecture, strong topical relevance, trustworthy evidence, entities, structured data and helpful answers.

Read the AI Search SEO service and the AEO FAQ and GEO FAQ to see how these concepts fit into a broader SEO strategy.

Frequently Asked SEO Questions

How do I choose an SEO consultant?

Start with the problem. Compare process, technical understanding, search-intent analysis, communication, evidence and realistic expectations. The phrase choose an seo consultant should lead to a comparison of fit, not a ranking promise.

Can an SEO consultant in Bangladesh work internationally?

Yes. International SEO requires market-specific keyword research, language and location targeting, technical configuration, content relevance and competitor analysis. My location in Dhaka does not limit the markets that a well-structured remote SEO project can serve.

What makes a professional SEO consultant different?

A professional SEO consultant should connect diagnosis to implementation. Instead of producing a generic checklist, the work should explain the cause, impact, priority and next action. That is the standard behind my SEO audits and strategy resources.

Do you work with Shopify, Wix and WordPress?

Platform changes the implementation details, not the SEO principles. I can structure consulting around Shopify, Wix, WordPress and custom HTML environments, with attention to crawlability, indexability, templates, metadata, internal links, structured data and content architecture.
