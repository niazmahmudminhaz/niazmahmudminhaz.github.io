Common SEO Mistakes That Create More Work Than Growth

  1. Keyword stuffing: forcing the same phrase into every section instead of satisfying intent.
  2. Duplicate pages: publishing near-identical URLs for tiny keyword variations.
  3. Ignoring crawl paths: assuming a page is discoverable because a human can find it somewhere.
  4. Weak internal links: isolating important pages instead of creating contextual relationships.
  5. Thin service pages: describing a service without explaining who it is for, what is included or why it matters.
  6. Buying low-quality links: optimizing for link count instead of relevance and authority.
  7. Changing everything after one ranking drop: treating volatility as proof of one specific problem.
  8. Reporting vanity metrics: focusing on screenshots rather than qualified traffic, important pages and business outcomes.
Better rule: diagnose the system, solve the highest-impact problem and document why the change should help.
