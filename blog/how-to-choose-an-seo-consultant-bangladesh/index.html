Start with the problem, not the sales pitch

Begin with the problem you need solved: weak local visibility, poor indexing, unclear keyword targeting, an ecommerce catalog problem, a traffic drop or an international launch. A consultant should understand the problem before prescribing a tactic.

A useful first conversation is about business goals, target markets, important pages and available evidence. A generic promise to rank keywords quickly is not a diagnosis.

Test technical and search-intent depth

Ask how the consultant would investigate crawlability, indexation, canonical URLs, redirects, sitemaps, internal links and page structure. Then ask how they would determine which page should target a specific query.

A good answer connects technical evidence with search intent. It should show that the consultant understands how page type, content, architecture and business goals interact.

Look for Bangladesh-specific understanding

Bangladesh-facing work may involve local service areas, mixed English and Bangla search behavior, ecommerce, Dhaka neighbourhood intent or businesses that later expand internationally. The strategy should reflect the real market instead of simply inserting “Bangladesh” into every page.

For local SEO, accurate Business Profile information, real service areas and trustworthy references matter. Google describes local visibility through relevance, distance and prominence, so fixed Maps positions should never be promised as a product.

Compare evidence, scope, ownership and ethics

Case studies should explain context, implementation and measurement. Certifications can show learning, but they do not replace evidence of problem solving. Also clarify who owns accounts, who implements changes, what is delivered and how communication works.

Avoid guaranteed rankings, bulk low-quality links, fake reviews and vague monthly task lists. A professional consultant should be able to say what is unnecessary for your website as well as what is useful.

Practical checklist

  • Ask how your site would be diagnosed
  • Request evidence with context and dates
  • Clarify technical and search-intent knowledge
  • Confirm implementation responsibilities
  • Review reporting and measurement
  • Understand account ownership and contract terms

Questions people usually ask

Should I hire a freelancer, consultant or agency?

Compare the actual scope, complexity, implementation capacity and communication model rather than relying on the job label.

Can a Bangladesh SEO consultant work internationally?

Yes, when the consultant understands market-specific search intent, localization, multilingual architecture and international SEO requirements.

What should I ask before signing?

Ask what will be audited, what will be delivered, who implements changes, how progress is measured and what the consultant will not promise.
