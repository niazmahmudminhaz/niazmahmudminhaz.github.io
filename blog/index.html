What changed in Google Search during 2026, and what did not?

Google's current documentation continues to emphasize useful, original, people-first content and the core SEO fundamentals that make pages crawlable, understandable and trustworthy. At the same time, Google has published new guidance for AI search features, preferred sources, image metadata and platform content performance. The lesson is simple: new interfaces do not replace strong fundamentals.
