Get the identity and business facts right

Use the real business information, accurate category, current hours and legitimate contact details. The profile should match the business customers encounter in the real world.

Do not rewrite the business name with search keywords just to make it look more relevant. Local optimization should strengthen clarity, not alter identity.

Choose categories and services with restraint

Primary and secondary categories should accurately describe the business. Service information should help customers understand what is actually offered.

More categories are not automatically better. Irrelevant categories can dilute the profile's meaning and make the business less clear.

Use authentic photos and connect the right website page

Current photos can help customers understand the business. A strong website then provides the detailed service and location information the profile cannot fully contain.

Where a specific service or location page is the best destination, use it rather than always sending users to the homepage. The profile and website should reinforce the same entity.

Earn genuine reviews and measure actions

Real customer reviews can provide useful trust and prominence signals. Build a process that encourages honest feedback after genuine interactions, without fabrication or manipulation.

Measure what users do next: website visits, calls, enquiries, bookings or sales. A rise in profile views without useful actions is incomplete evidence.

Practical checklist

  • Verify business identity
  • Select accurate categories
  • Keep hours and contact information current
  • Add genuine business photos
  • Link to the strongest website destination
  • Encourage honest customer feedback

Questions people usually ask

How often should a Business Profile be updated?

Update it whenever real business information changes. Accuracy is more important than artificial activity.

Should I add every possible service?

Only services the business genuinely provides. Relevance and clarity beat exhaustive keyword lists.

Should the profile link to the homepage?

Only when that is the most useful destination. A relevant service or location page can be better.
