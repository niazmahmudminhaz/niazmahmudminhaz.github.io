How this fits the site

The Strategy Lab supports the main silo. It does not replace it.

The primary commercial architecture is the Services hub, where the defined service families live. The Blog handles educational intent, Case Studies hold evidence and investigations, About carries professional identity and certifications, and Contact handles qualified enquiries.

This lab is a supporting research layer. It can connect a specialized topic to the appropriate service or Blog resource when that relationship is genuine. It should not become a second services directory or a place for thin keyword pages.

What trustworthy SEO looks like here

  • Clear authorship and professional positioning without invented credentials.
  • Useful content that answers the searcher's problem before pushing a sale.
  • Commercial pages that explain scope, process, limitations and fit.
  • Case studies that distinguish illustrative examples from verified first-party evidence.
  • Technical recommendations tied to actual website conditions.
  • AI-search readiness based on entities, evidence, clarity and structure rather than unsupported visibility promises.
  • Internal linking that helps people discover the next useful resource.
One rule: more URLs do not automatically mean more authority. Better coverage, better evidence and better user experience make additional pages worthwhile.

The Strategy Lab exists to organize supporting research and specialized resources. It should strengthen the website's information architecture, not compete with the Services or Blog hubs.

Where to start

Businesses should start with the Services hub and choose the closest legitimate commercial service. Readers seeking evidence can visit Case Studies. Readers seeking education should use the Blog. Professional identity and certifications belong on About.

Need a site-specific view? Use the consultation path to turn a real SEO problem into a prioritized, evidence-led plan.
