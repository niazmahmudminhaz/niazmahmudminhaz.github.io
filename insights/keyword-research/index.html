Keyword Research: From Search Terms to Page-Level Intent

Keyword research is the bridge between what a business offers and what people actually search. The goal is not to collect the largest possible list. The goal is to understand intent, prioritize useful queries and map them to the right pages.

My Keyword Research Framework

  1. Start with the business offer and audience.
  2. Identify primary commercial and informational intents.
  3. Expand into long-tail, semantic, local and question-based queries.
  4. Review competitors and SERP page types.
  5. Cluster terms by intent and assign one primary destination.
  6. Connect related pages with internal links.

Why Search Intent Matters

A keyword can look attractive while having the wrong intent for a service page. A commercial service query should normally lead to a relevant service page, while a learning query can lead to an article or FAQ. This separation prevents keyword cannibalization and keeps content useful.

Keyword Mapping and Internal Linking

Use On-Page SEO to turn research into page structure. Then connect the service page to supporting resources such as Content SEO and Competitive Analysis.
