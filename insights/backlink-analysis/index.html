Backlink Analysis: Evaluate Authority Before Building Links

Backlink analysis asks a simple question: which external sites reference a website, why do they reference it, and what does that pattern tell us about authority and relevance?

Key Backlink Signals

  • Relevance of the referring site and page
  • Quality and trust of the referring domain
  • Context around the link
  • Natural variation in anchor text
  • Competitor link gaps
  • Suspicious or irrelevant patterns

Competitor Backlink Analysis

Competitor links can reveal industry directories, useful publications, partnerships and content that naturally earns references. The point is to identify legitimate opportunities, not reproduce every link a competitor has.

For local businesses, backlink research should connect with citations and Google Business Profile strategy. See Local SEO citations and GBP and Off-Page SEO.
