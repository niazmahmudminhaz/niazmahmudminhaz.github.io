Competitive Analysis: Find the Gaps Your Website Can Own

Competitive analysis helps explain why competing pages may be visible for a query and where your website has a realistic opportunity. It is not about copying competitors. It is about understanding the search environment.

What I Review

  • Search intent and ranking page types
  • Topic coverage and content depth
  • Page structure and internal linking
  • Title and heading patterns
  • Local relevance where applicable
  • Backlink and authority gaps
  • Content formats and SERP features

Turn Research Into Actions

The output should be a prioritized set of opportunities. Some gaps belong on a service page, some need a supporting article, and others belong in a technical or authority workstream. This connects competitive analysis to SEO audits and Off-Page SEO.
