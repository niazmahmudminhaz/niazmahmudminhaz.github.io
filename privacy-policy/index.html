Privacy

Information You Choose to Provide

When you request an SEO consultation or contact me, you may voluntarily provide details such as your name, email address, website URL, business context and a description of the SEO problem you want reviewed. I use the information you submit to understand your request, communicate with you and discuss relevant SEO services.

How Website Information Is Used

Information is used for practical website and communication purposes, such as responding to enquiries, reviewing an SEO request, maintaining site functionality, improving the visitor experience and understanding which website resources are useful.

I do not use this website as a place to publicly display your private contact information or your consultation details.

Website Technology

This website is delivered as a static website and may rely on standard hosting, form-processing, security and analytics technologies. These providers may process limited technical information when necessary to deliver the requested function.

Cookies, Local Storage and Preferences

The website theme preference may be stored locally in your browser so the interface can remember your selected display mode. Where analytics or similar services are enabled, those services may use cookies or related technologies according to their own documentation and settings.

Data Sharing and Third-Party Processing

Information is not presented as being sold to advertisers. When a third-party service is required to process a form, host the website, protect the site or provide analytics, that provider may process the information necessary for its function. Each provider remains responsible for its own privacy practices.

How Long Information Is Kept

Contact information is kept only as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, maintain useful communication and meet applicable operational or legal needs. Retention can vary depending on the nature of the request.

Your Choices

  • You can stop further communication by requesting that contact cease.
  • You can manage browser storage, cookies and related permissions through your browser settings.
