SEO Audit Demo: Niaz Mahmud Minhaz Website

This is a demonstration and practice audit of my own website. It is not a client engagement, and no client results are claimed.

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Scope

This teardown reviews the site's committed HTML, navigation, URL structure, metadata, internal linking, structured-data implementation and crawl directives. Live performance metrics, backlink indexes and search-console data are outside this source review unless explicitly available.

Finding 1: Keyword stuffing

Several pages used repeated exact-match phrases in a way that weakened readability. The remediation is to retain the underlying business point while using fewer target phrases naturally and avoiding long bolded keyword sequences.

Finding 2: Orphaned Insights content

The Insights section existed but was not consistently exposed through the primary navigation. Adding an Insights item creates a clearer path between informational content and service pages.

Finding 3: Broken certification verification presentation

Certification entries contained placeholder verification destinations. A public credential link is used only where a working verification destination exists. Entries without a public verification URL are presented without a dead link.

Finding 4: AI-search readiness

The site can improve extractability through concise direct answers, consistent terminology, structured data that matches visible content, clear entity information and accessible pages. These are visibility-supporting practices, not guarantees of inclusion or ranking in AI answers.

Finding 5: Information architecture

Service pages, research pages and FAQs should reinforce one another through descriptive internal links. Dedicated AEO and GEO service pages are stronger destinations than routing users through FAQ fragments alone.

What I would measure next

  • Index coverage and crawl findings in Google Search Console
  • Organic queries and landing-page performance
  • Core Web Vitals and real-user performance
  • Backlink and referring-domain quality
  • Internal-link coverage and orphan-page checks
  • Structured-data validation
