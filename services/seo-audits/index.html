Complete SEO Audit for Websites That Need Clear Priorities

A useful SEO audit should explain what is wrong, why it matters and what to fix first. I review the website as a connected system and organize findings by impact and implementation priority.

What a Comprehensive SEO Audit Covers

  • Technical SEO and crawlability
  • Indexability, redirects and canonical signals
  • On-page SEO and title/meta quality
  • Keyword targeting and search intent
  • Content depth and topical relevance
  • Internal linking and site architecture
  • Local SEO signals where relevant
  • Backlink profile and off-page risks
  • Structured data and search appearance
  • Performance and Core Web Vitals

Audit Outcome
