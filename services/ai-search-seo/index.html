AI Search SEO, AEO and GEO for Modern Search Visibility

AI search SEO focuses on making a website's information easier for modern search systems and answer experiences to interpret. I connect AI search work to technical SEO, useful content, structured data, entities, internal linking and evidence rather than treating AI visibility as a separate shortcut.

AI Search SEO Areas

  • AI search content structure
  • Answer-focused content and AEO
  • Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)
  • Entity clarity and topical relationships
  • Structured data and machine-readable context
  • Question-led FAQ content
  • Content evidence and trust signals

AEO: Answer Engine Optimization

AEO organizes useful information so direct-answer systems can identify concise answers and supporting context. Strong AEO still depends on crawlable pages, clear headings, accurate information and a useful user experience.

GEO: Generative Engine Optimization

GEO focuses on visibility in generative search experiences. It should build on the same fundamentals that support organic search: expertise, helpful content, entity clarity, structured information and trustworthy sources.

AI Search Is Not a Replacement for SEO

Technical SEO, on-page SEO, content SEO and authority remain foundational. AI search SEO adds another visibility layer to the system. See the AEO FAQ and GEO FAQ for practical questions.
