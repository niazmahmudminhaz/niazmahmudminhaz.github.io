How the Service Silo Works

The Services hub is the commercial root. A visitor can move from the business problem to the most relevant service, then into supporting Blog content, Case Studies or the consultation path. Related services are linked where the relationship is genuine, rather than every page linking to everything.

Example: a technical problem can lead from SEO Audits to Technical SEO; an answer-focused content need can lead from AEO to AI Search SEO or GEO; a platform project can move from Shopify SEO or Webflow SEO into Technical SEO when the broader issue requires it.

Choosing the Right Starting Point

Start with SEO Consultation when the problem is unclear. Start with SEO Audits when the site needs a structured diagnosis. Choose a focused service when the problem is already understood. Use the platform pages when implementation depends on WordPress, Shopify, WooCommerce, Webflow, Wix or Squarespace. Use Hyperlocal or International SEO when geography materially changes the search strategy.

Evidence, Trust and Expectations

Every service should describe what can actually be reviewed or improved. No service page promises rankings, guaranteed traffic, guaranteed indexing or guaranteed AI citations. Recommendations should be grounded in technical inspection, search intent, first-party data, documented research or clearly labeled assumptions.
