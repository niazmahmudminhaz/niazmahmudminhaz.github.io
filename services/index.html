How the Service Silo Works

The Services hub is the commercial root. Visitors can move from a business problem to one of the 22 services, then into a supporting article, Case Study or consultation path. Related services are linked when the relationship is genuine, rather than forcing every page to link to everything.

Examples: SEO Audits → Technical SEO; On-Page SEO → Content SEO and Keyword Research; Local SEO → Hyperlocal SEO; Off-Page SEO → Backlink Analysis; AI Search SEO → AEO, GEO and GBO; Ecommerce SEO → the relevant platform service.

Choosing the Right Starting Point

Start with SEO Consultation when the problem is unclear. Start with SEO Audits when the site needs a structured diagnosis. Choose a focused service when the problem is already understood. Use the platform services when implementation depends on Shopify, WooCommerce, WordPress, Webflow, Wix or Squarespace. Use Hyperlocal or International SEO when geography materially changes the search strategy.

Evidence, Trust and Expectations

Services describe what can actually be reviewed or improved. No service page promises rankings, guaranteed traffic, guaranteed indexing or guaranteed AI citations. Recommendations should be grounded in technical inspection, search intent, first-party data, documented research or clearly labeled assumptions.
