AI Search · GEO

Generative Engine Optimization for Modern Search Experiences

GEO focuses on making information clearer and more useful for generative search experiences. I approach GEO through the same foundations that support organic search: useful content, topical relevance, entities, evidence, structured information and strong technical accessibility.

What GEO Covers

  • Entity and topic clarity
  • Evidence-led content structure
  • Question and intent mapping
  • Supporting facts and contextual explanations
  • Structured data and machine-readable relationships
  • Internal linking and topical clusters
  • AI search content readiness

GEO Without Empty Promises

Generative search visibility cannot be guaranteed by stuffing a page with AI-related terms. The stronger approach is to make information understandable, useful, specific and supported by credible signals.

Connect GEO with AI Search SEO, Technical SEO, On-Page SEO and Content SEO.

Common GEO Questions

What is GEO?

Generative Engine Optimization is a search strategy focused on improving how useful, well-structured information can be understood and surfaced in generative search experiences.

Does GEO replace SEO?

No. GEO depends on many of the same fundamentals as SEO, including crawlability, useful content, topical relevance, internal linking and trustworthy information.
