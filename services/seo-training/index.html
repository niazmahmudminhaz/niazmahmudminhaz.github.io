SEO Training

Practical SEO Training Built Around How Search Actually Works

SEO training should teach a repeatable method, not a list of disconnected tactics. This training path focuses on diagnosing websites, mapping search intent and turning findings into practical implementation steps.

Training Modules

Technical SEO

Crawlability, rendering, indexability, sitemaps, canonicals, redirects and structured data.

Keyword Research

Business-first keywords, intent, long-tail queries, topic clusters and page mapping.

On-Page & Content

Titles, headings, semantic relevance, content structure and internal linking.

Local SEO

Google Business Profile, citations, location pages and local search journeys.

Measurement

Search Console, Analytics, evidence, reporting and prioritization.

AI Search

AI search, AEO and GEO as extensions of strong SEO fundamentals.

Who This Is For

The curriculum can support beginners, website owners, marketers, junior SEO practitioners and small teams that want to understand why an SEO change should be made before they implement it.

How the Training Is Structured

  1. Start with how search engines crawl, render and index a page, so later modules build on a correct foundation.
  2. Work through each module using real page examples rather than abstract theory alone.
  3. Practice diagnosing an actual website issue and connecting it to the right fix.
  4. Review reporting and measurement so progress can be checked with Search Console and Analytics data instead of assumptions.

What This Training Does Not Promise

This is a practical, method-focused training path. It does not promise guaranteed rankings, traffic numbers or a fixed timeline, since outcomes depend on the website, the industry and how consistently the material is applied.
