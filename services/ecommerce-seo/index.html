E-commerce SEO

E-commerce SEO That Connects Product Discovery to Search Intent

E-commerce SEO needs more than ranking a homepage. The important search journeys often begin on product, category, collection, comparison and informational pages. I build SEO plans around those page types and the business intent behind them.

Core E-commerce SEO Areas

Product Pages

Improve titles, descriptions, headings, media context, internal links and structured information without duplicating manufacturer copy.

Category Pages

Map broader commercial intent to useful category pages and make navigation easy for both shoppers and crawlers.

Technical SEO

Review crawl paths, faceted URLs, canonicals, pagination, sitemaps, status codes and indexation patterns.

Search Intent

Separate research, comparison and purchase intent so each query has an appropriate landing page.

Internal Linking

Connect categories, products and editorial resources so important URLs receive contextual links.

Measurement

Use Search Console and Analytics evidence to identify pages with high demand, low CTR or weak engagement.

Platform and Market Fit

The implementation differs between Shopify, WordPress, custom e-commerce systems and other platforms. The strategy also changes with Bangladesh, regional or international targeting. This page connects to dedicated Shopify SEO and WordPress SEO resources.
