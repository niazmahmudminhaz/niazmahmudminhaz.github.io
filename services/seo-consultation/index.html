SEO Consultation

SEO Consultation for Clearer Decisions and Better Priorities

An SEO consultation is useful when you know something is wrong but do not yet know which SEO problem deserves attention first. I use the website, search intent, technical signals, available first-party data and business context to identify practical next steps.

When a Consultation Makes Sense

  • Organic visibility has changed and you need to understand why.
  • You are unsure whether the problem is technical, content, local or authority-related.
  • You are planning a redesign, migration or CMS change.
  • You need a second opinion on an SEO audit or agency recommendation.
  • You want to prioritize a backlog before spending development or content resources.
  • You need a focused view of AI search, AEO or GEO opportunities without abandoning SEO fundamentals.

What We Can Review

Depending on scope, the consultation can cover crawlability, rendering, indexability, canonicalization, internal linking, page intent, content quality, local search, ecommerce architecture, platform-specific SEO, backlinks, measurement and AI-search readiness. The exact focus should match the decision you are trying to make.

Evidence Before Recommendations

I separate observed facts from hypotheses. A Search Console pattern, a broken internal link, an incorrect canonical or a duplicated page template is evidence. A possible ranking cause that has not been verified is a hypothesis. That distinction keeps the consultation practical and reduces unnecessary work.

What You Receive

A focused consultation can result in a prioritized action list, implementation questions for a developer or writer, an audit direction, a page-level recommendation, a migration checklist or a decision framework. The goal is clarity, not a long generic task list.

Connected SEO Services

A consultation may lead into SEO Audits, Technical SEO, On-Page SEO, Local SEO, Ecommerce SEO or AI Search SEO when a deeper engagement is justified.

Need a second opinion? Bring the website, the business goal and the SEO question. I can help turn the issue into a prioritized next step.
