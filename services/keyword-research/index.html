Keyword Research

Keyword research is the process of connecting a real business offer with the language people use when searching. The goal is not a huge list of phrases. The goal is a prioritized set of queries that can be assigned to the right pages and decisions.

Focus: business relevance, search intent, long-tail discovery, semantic relationships, competitive SERP patterns and page-level mapping.

What I Analyze

I start from the business, audience, products or services and target markets. Then I build keyword groups around commercial, transactional, informational and navigational needs where relevant. Local, geographic, question-based and long-tail variations are considered when they represent genuine demand.

Search Intent Comes First

A keyword should not be mapped only because its volume looks attractive. I review the likely search intent and the page types that satisfy it. A service query belongs on a suitable service page; a learning query may belong in a Blog or research resource. This separation helps prevent competing pages from chasing the same intent.

Keyword Mapping Workflow

  1. Define the business offer, audience, geography and goals.
  2. Collect seed topics and expand them into relevant query families.
  3. Classify intent and inspect current search result patterns.
  4. Cluster related queries and identify one primary destination per intent.
  5. Map keywords to existing pages or identify genuine content gaps.
  6. Connect the resulting map to internal linking, content planning and measurement.

Deliverables

Depending on scope, the work can include a prioritized keyword set, intent classifications, topical clusters, page-to-keyword mapping, location modifiers, content-gap notes and an internal-linking plan. The output is designed to help you decide what pages should exist, which pages need improvement and where not to create unnecessary duplicates.

How It Connects to the Service Silo

Keyword Research supports On-Page SEO, Content SEO, Local SEO, Competitive Analysis and SEO Audits. Research about search behavior remains in Search Behavior; explanatory guides belong in the Blog.

Evidence-Led Expectations

Keyword research informs strategy. It does not guarantee rankings, traffic or conversions. Search demand changes, competitors change and search results evolve, so the map should be treated as a decision framework that is validated over time.
