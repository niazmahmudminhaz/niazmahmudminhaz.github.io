Search performance

Google Search Console

Google Search Console case study demonstrates a data-driven SEO process focused on improving organic search performance, search visibility, indexing, and qualified traffic.

Using Google Search Console, I analyzed total clicks, impressions, average CTR, average position, indexing status, search queries, landing pages, and Core Web Vitals to identify measurable SEO opportunities. The analysis helped uncover keyword gaps, pages with high impressions but low CTR, indexing issues, and opportunities to strengthen search intent alignment.

I used these insights to refine on-page SEO, title tags, meta descriptions, internal linking, technical SEO, and content targeting. The analysis reflects a structured SEO audit and performance analysis rather than assumptions. Google Search Console data provides the foundation for evaluating how effectively a website is crawled, indexed, and discovered in Google Search, supporting sustainable organic growth through evidence-based SEO strategy.

Website analytics

Google Analytics 4

Google Analytics 4 case study demonstrates how I use analytics data to evaluate SEO performance beyond rankings and traffic alone.

Using GA4, I analyzed users, sessions, engagement rate, engaged sessions, landing pages, traffic acquisition, organic search traffic, user journeys, and key conversions to understand how visitors interact with the website. The analysis connected SEO activity with measurable business outcomes, helping identify which landing pages attract qualified organic visitors and which areas require stronger search intent alignment, content optimization, technical SEO, or conversion improvements.

I used these insights alongside Google Search Console data to build a more complete SEO performance picture, combining search visibility with on-site behavior. This evidence-based approach supports E-E-A-T principles, transparent reporting, and continuous SEO optimization. GA4 helps transform raw website performance data into actionable insights that guide content strategy, technical decisions, conversion optimization, and sustainable organic growth. This makes reporting clearer, more accountable, and strategically useful.

SEO strategy

How I Turn SEO Data Into Decisions

SEO is a system. Technical SEO helps search engines crawl and understand a site. On-page SEO aligns pages with search intent. Content SEO builds topical relevance. Local SEO strengthens geographic signals. Off-page SEO develops authority. AEO and GEO improve the clarity and extractability of useful information for modern search experiences.

  • Clear thinking: understand the problem before recommending a fix.
  • Actionable SEO: turn findings into prioritized implementation steps.
  • Evidence: distinguish measured results from assumptions.
  • Credibility through evidence: explain the source, date range and limitations of important metrics.
  • Business alignment: connect search visibility to useful pages, qualified traffic and commercial intent.

Case study framework

My Four-Step SEO Framework

The same evidence-led workflow can be applied to a new website, local business, e-commerce store, SaaS company, agency site or international service business.

01

Baseline

Establish the current technical condition, organic visibility, important pages, query patterns and measurement setup.

02

Diagnosis

Find crawlability, indexability, relevance, content, internal-linking, local and authority gaps.

03

Implementation

Prioritize changes by impact, effort and business importance instead of making random SEO edits.

04

Measurement

Compare appropriate time periods and metrics, document changes and identify the next optimization cycle.

Trust and methodology

What Counts as SEO Evidence?

First-party data means measurement from a property or analytics setup that I control or am authorized to access. Client evidence means information shared with permission and enough context to interpret it responsibly. Illustrative material is clearly labeled as a reporting or educational visual.

I will not present invented traffic, fabricated reviews, unsupported ranking claims, anonymous success stories or a demonstration dashboard as proof of a client outcome. This protects the integrity of the portfolio and makes the case studies more useful to serious clients.
