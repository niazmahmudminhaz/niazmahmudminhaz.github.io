About

Niaz Mahmud Minhaz

I’m Niaz Mahmud Minhaz, an SEO Consultant from Bangladesh, focused on helping websites become more discoverable through clear analysis, practical strategy and evidence-based SEO. I focus on understanding the business and search intent before recommending changes, rather than applying the same SEO checklist to every website.

Clear Thinking

Good SEO starts with understanding the problem.

I analyze the business, website, search intent and available data before deciding what needs to change. My approach combines technical diagnosis, content relevance, internal linking, local signals, authority research, measurement and clear prioritization.

Actionable SEO

SEO insights are only useful when they can be turned into action.

I focus on practical recommendations that can be understood, prioritized and implemented. Every service has its own dedicated page, allowing each area of SEO to be explored in greater detail without reducing the entire process to a generic list of services.

Proof Over Promises

I believe SEO should be built on evidence, not exaggerated promises.

I use data, technical findings, search signals and measurable observations to understand opportunities and guide decisions. The objective is to identify what can realistically be improved and why it matters.

Credibility Through Evidence

My academic path in Data Science and Engineering complements my interest in measurement, analysis and evidence-based decision-making.

I am building a career around helping websites become more discoverable while continuing to deepen my technical and analytical skills through practical SEO work, analytics training and ongoing study.

Signals Beyond Your Website

Search visibility is not determined by a website alone.

I also consider the broader signals surrounding a business, including local presence, authority, structured information, references and other factors that can contribute to how a business is understood across search.

Who I Help
