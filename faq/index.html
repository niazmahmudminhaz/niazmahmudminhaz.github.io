SEO Questions and Answers

What does an SEO consultant do?

An SEO consultant analyzes a website, its audience and its search competition, then recommends and prioritizes improvements that can increase organic visibility and qualified traffic.

How does technical SEO affect rankings?

Technical SEO helps search engines access, render, understand and index a website. Problems with crawling, indexing, architecture or performance can limit how effectively useful content can be discovered and evaluated.

What is a local SEO consultant?

A local SEO consultant helps businesses improve visibility for searches with geographic intent through local relevance, Google Business Profile strategy, citations, local pages and useful location-focused content.

How often should a website have an SEO audit?

There is no universal schedule. A major redesign, migration, traffic change, indexing problem or strategic shift can justify an audit. Smaller websites can also benefit from periodic checks as the site grows.

How long does SEO take to work?

SEO results vary by competition, website condition, content quality, authority, market and implementation speed. A responsible consultant should avoid guaranteeing a fixed ranking date.

Can you work with businesses outside Bangladesh?

Yes. Remote SEO consulting can support international businesses when the strategy accounts for each target market, language, location and search intent.

What is AEO and GEO?

AEO refers to Answer Engine Optimization, while GEO commonly refers to Generative Engine Optimization. Both focus on making useful information easier for answer engines and generative search experiences to understand and surface.
