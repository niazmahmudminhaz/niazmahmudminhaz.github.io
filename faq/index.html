Frequently Asked Questions

SEO Questions, Answered Clearly

Clear answers to common questions about SEO consulting, technical foundations, local visibility, AI search and working with an independent SEO consultant. Each answer is kept focused, practical and connected to the relevant service where useful.

How do I choose an SEO consultant?

Compare the consultant's process, technical understanding, search intent analysis, evidence, communication and realistic expectations against your actual website problem.

What does a technical SEO consultant do?

A technical SEO consultant reviews crawling, rendering, indexing, architecture, performance, redirects, canonical signals, structured data and other technical foundations.

What does a local SEO consultant do?

A local SEO consultant improves local relevance through Google Business Profile strategy, citations, location pages, local content, keyword research and competitor analysis.

Explore Local SEO →

What should I look for in a strong local SEO consultant?

A strong local strategy should connect Google Business Profile work, local keyword research, citations, location pages, internal linking and useful local content. The right consultant should evaluate those areas against the actual service area and business goals.

How does technical SEO affect rankings?

Technical SEO helps search engines access, render, understand and index a website. Problems with crawling, indexing, architecture or performance can limit how effectively useful content is discovered and evaluated.

What is an SEO audit?

An SEO audit diagnoses technical, on-page, content, local and authority issues and turns them into prioritized actions.

Explore SEO Audits →

What is AEO?

AEO means Answer Engine Optimization. It focuses on clear, useful answers and supporting context that answer-focused systems can interpret. AEO should complement technical SEO, content quality and information architecture.

Explore AEO →

What is GEO?

GEO commonly means Generative Engine Optimization. It focuses on visibility in generative search experiences. Strong GEO builds on crawlability, helpful content, entities, structured data, evidence and trust rather than shortcuts.

Explore GEO →

What is AI Search SEO?

AI Search SEO connects traditional SEO fundamentals with the information needs of modern AI-powered search experiences.

Explore AI Search SEO →

Can you work with Shopify, Wix and WordPress?

Yes. Platform changes implementation details, but SEO principles remain focused on crawlability, indexability, templates, metadata, internal links, structured data and useful content.

Can an SEO consultant in Bangladesh work with international businesses?

Yes. Remote SEO can support international businesses when the strategy accounts for target markets, language, location, competition and search intent.

How long does SEO take?

Results vary by competition, website condition, content quality, authority, market and implementation speed. A responsible consultant should avoid guaranteeing a fixed ranking date.

Do you offer SEO for specific industries?

Consulting can be adapted to e-commerce, local services and specialist niches. The SEO approach is based on the site's market, search intent and technical and content requirements.

How much does SEO cost, and why does it vary?

SEO pricing varies with site size, technical complexity, competition, number of markets, content requirements, implementation scope and the level of ongoing support. A responsible quote should follow a clear diagnosis and defined scope rather than a one-size-fits-all price.

What's the difference between hiring a consultant vs. an agency?

A consultant typically provides direct specialist strategy and hands-on guidance, while an agency may offer a broader team and wider delivery capacity. The better option depends on the complexity of the work, required services, communication needs and budget.

Can you guarantee first-page rankings?

No ethical SEO practitioner can guarantee a specific first-page ranking. Search results depend on competition, search intent, algorithms, website changes and other factors outside a consultant's control, so SEO should be measured through documented work and meaningful performance signals rather than guaranteed positions.
