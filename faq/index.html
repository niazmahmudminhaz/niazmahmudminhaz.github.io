SEO Questions, Answered Clearly

Clear answers to common questions about SEO consulting, technical foundations, local visibility, AI search and working with an independent SEO consultant.

How do I choose an SEO consultant?

Compare the consultant's process, technical understanding, search intent analysis, evidence, communication and realistic expectations against your actual website problem.

What does a technical SEO consultant do?

A technical SEO consultant reviews crawling, rendering, indexing, architecture, performance, redirects, canonical signals, structured data and other technical foundations.

What does a local SEO consultant do?

A local SEO consultant improves local relevance through Google Business Profile strategy, citations, location pages, local content, keyword research and competitor analysis.

What is an SEO audit?

An SEO audit diagnoses technical, on-page, content, local and authority issues and turns them into prioritized actions.

What is AEO?

AEO means Answer Engine Optimization. It focuses on clear, useful answers and supporting context that answer-focused systems can interpret.

What is GEO?

GEO commonly means Generative Engine Optimization. It focuses on visibility in generative search experiences and builds on crawlability, helpful content, entities, structured data, evidence and trust.

What is AI Search SEO?

AI Search SEO connects traditional SEO fundamentals with the information needs of modern AI-powered search experiences.

Can an SEO consultant in Bangladesh work with international businesses?

Yes. Remote SEO can support international businesses when the strategy accounts for target markets, language, location, competition and search intent.

How long does SEO take?

Results vary by competition, website condition, content quality, authority, market and implementation speed. A responsible consultant should avoid guaranteeing a fixed ranking date.

Can you guarantee first-page rankings?

No ethical SEO practitioner can guarantee a specific first-page ranking. SEO should be measured through documented work and meaningful performance signals rather than guaranteed positions.

Why You Need SEO
