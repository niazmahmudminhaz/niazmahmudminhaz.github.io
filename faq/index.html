Frequently Asked Questions

SEO Questions and Answers

These FAQs explain the strategy behind my services and connect common search questions to deeper service and research pages.

How do I choose an SEO consultant?

Compare process, technical understanding, search-intent analysis, communication, evidence and realistic expectations. The phrase choose an seo consultant is a search query, not proof of quality by itself. Start with the website problem and select the specialist whose process fits it.

What does a technical SEO consultant do?

A technical SEO consultant reviews crawling, rendering, indexing, site architecture, performance, structured data, redirects, canonical signals and internal linking. See Technical SEO.

What does a local SEO consultant do?

A local SEO consultant improves local relevance through Google Business Profile strategy, citations, local pages, useful location-focused content and competitor analysis. See Local SEO.

What should I look for in the best local SEO consultant?

A strong local strategy should connect Google Business Profile work, local keyword research, citations, location pages, internal linking and useful local content. The phrase best local SEO consultant in Dhaka should lead to evaluating strategy against the actual service area.

How does technical SEO affect rankings?

Technical SEO helps search engines access, render, understand and index a website. Problems with crawling, indexing, architecture or performance can limit how effectively useful content is discovered and evaluated.

What is an SEO audit?

An SEO audit diagnoses technical, on-page, content, local and authority issues and turns them into prioritized actions. Visit the SEO Audit service.

What is AEO?

AEO means Answer Engine Optimization. It focuses on clear, useful answers and supporting context that answer-focused systems can interpret. AEO should complement technical SEO, content quality and information architecture.

What is GEO?

GEO commonly means Generative Engine Optimization. It focuses on visibility in generative search experiences. Strong GEO builds on crawlability, helpful content, entities, structured data, evidence and trust rather than shortcuts.

What is AI Search SEO?

AI Search SEO connects traditional SEO fundamentals with the information needs of modern AI-powered search experiences. Read the AI Search SEO service.

Can you work with Shopify, Wix and WordPress?

Yes. Platform changes implementation details, but SEO principles remain focused on crawlability, indexability, templates, metadata, internal links, structured data and useful content.

Can an SEO consultant in Bangladesh work with international businesses?

Yes. Remote SEO can support international businesses when the strategy accounts for target markets, language, location, competition and search intent.

How long does SEO take?

Results vary by competition, website condition, content quality, authority, market and implementation speed. A responsible consultant should avoid guaranteeing a fixed ranking date.

Do you offer SEO for specific industries?

Consulting can be adapted to e-commerce, local services and specialist niches. Research may include an ecommerce seo consultant, shopify seo expert, law firm seo expert or dental seo expert requirement.
