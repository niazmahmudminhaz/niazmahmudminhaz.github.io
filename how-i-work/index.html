Process

How I Work

I use a diagnosis-first SEO process. The aim is to understand the business and search intent, identify the highest-value problems, turn findings into a practical roadmap, and measure what changes.

Last updated: August 17, 2026

1. Discovery

I start by understanding the business, audience, market, website structure and search objectives. This creates the context needed to distinguish important SEO problems from symptoms.

2. Audit

I review technical foundations, crawling and indexing, page relevance, search intent, internal linking, content, local signals and authority where relevant. Findings are tied to evidence rather than assumptions.

3. Roadmap

Audit findings become prioritized actions. I separate critical technical issues from content and authority opportunities so implementation can follow a clear order.

4. Implementation

Depending on the agreed scope, recommendations can be implemented directly or handed to the website owner or development team. Changes should be understandable, reviewable and reversible where practical.

5. Measurement

I use available search and website data to evaluate progress, identify new issues and refine priorities. SEO is an ongoing process, not a one-time checklist.

Principles

  • Clear thinking before recommendations
  • Actionable SEO instead of generic checklists
  • Proof over promises
  • Credibility through evidence
  • Signals beyond your website when they are relevant
